QUICK START & RESEARCH NOTES

User Guide

This single-file app runs locally or from static hosting. Public demonstrations use synthetic images and test identifiers.

Start an Experiment in Four Steps

1
Prepare Four Image Sets

In the Emotion Image Library, import Sets A/B/C/D. For each set, provide 10 neutral images for NEU-NAT, 10 negative images for NEG-NAT, and 10 different negative images for NEG-SUP. Match valence and arousal across sets as closely as possible.

2
Enter Session Details

Enter the Participant ID, Day, Exercise or Rest, Pre or Post, and the image set. All participants complete NEU-NAT, NEG-NAT and NEG-SUP. Counterbalance the order of NEG-NAT and NEG-SUP blocks across participants and assessments.

3
Launch the Full-Screen Emotion Task

Complete the NEU-NAT block first, followed by NEG-NAT and NEG-SUP in counterbalanced order. Each trial displays a fixation cross, an image, valence/arousal rating scales, and a recovery interval. Instructions appear before every block. Unanswered ratings remain blank.

4
Export Data

After finishing, open Ratings & Data to download Trial CSV (trial-level ratings) and Event CSV (timestamped event log). Back up approved research exports after each session. The condition column retains the legacy codes WNeu, WNeg and ES for compatibility.

Experiment & Data Quality Notes

• Default paradigm: 10 NEU-NAT + 10 NEG-NAT + 10 NEG-SUP images (30 trials total). Each trial comprises 2 s fixation + 8 s image + 8 s SAM rating + 20 s recovery, totalling approximately 19 minutes, excluding block instructions and breaks.

• NEU-NAT: View neutral images naturally.NEG-NAT: View negative images naturally, without intentionally regulating your emotions.NEG-SUP: When viewing negative images, suppress outward emotional expressions (facial and bodily) without deliberately changing your internal feelings. Give SAM ratings of your actual subjective experience in all three conditions.

• This application uses 1–5 SAM-style numerical ratings: Valence 1 = very unpleasant, 5 = very pleasant; Arousal 1 = very calm, 5 = highly aroused. The numeric buttons are a practical alternative interface and are NOT the validated standard SAM pictograms. If the study requires the standard SAM, replace this interface and verify the scale permissions and instructions.

• Original IAPS images are not embedded. The research team is responsible for obtaining and licensing stimuli. This version presents still images. Browser events document software state changes. Audio playback, hardware triggers and raw EEG/fNIRS acquisition require a separate implementation.

• During the experiment, use the Image size slider to resize the stimulus while preserving its aspect ratio. Set the same percentage for every participant to standardise visual angle. Skip Trial advances immediately to the next trial and marks the skipped trial in both CSV exports; partial SAM responses from a skipped trial are discarded.

• Switching to another tab, putting the computer to sleep, or reloading the page can affect timing and data integrity. Where possible, the app logs visibility_hidden and visibility_visible events, but cannot guarantee recovery of interrupted trials.
